# Project Mission, Vision, and Objectives

## 1. The Core Problem
In the current landscape of AI writing tools, the predominant model is "prompt-and-generate." Users provide a brief instruction, and the AI outputs a completed document. While fast, this approach fundamentally sidelines the human writer, resulting in homogenized voices, loss of nuance, and a disconnect between the author and the final argument. Existing tools treat AI as a **ghostwriter**.

## 2. Our Mission
Our mission is to build an AI-augmented writing and research environment that keeps the **human in the driver's seat**. We are building a system where the AI acts as a collaborative research partner, a specialized subject-matter expert, and a ruthless, constructive editor—never replacing the human author's core thesis or voice.

## 3. Core Philosophy & Tenets
* **Immutable Ground Truth:** Source materials (Resources) are sacred. They are read, highlighted, and analyzed, but never altered.
* **Curated Intelligence:** AI generates insights through specific "Lenses" (e.g., Financial Expert, Tech Lead). The human writer is the ultimate curator, deciding what context matters.
* **Block-Level Precision:** The editing phase treats the paragraph as the atomic unit of thought. Editing is a surgical process, not a bulk rewrite.
* **Frictionless Synthesis:** Bridging the gap between "a pile of notes" and a "first draft" is the hardest part of writing. The system automates this exact bridge, leaving the refinement to the user.

## 4. Target Audience
* **Senior Software Engineers & Architects:** Synthesizing technical specs, RFCs, and architecture documents from sprawling codebases and requirements.
* **Researchers & Analysts:** Distilling dozens of whitepapers into coherent market reports or academic summaries.
* **Content Creators & Authors:** Managing complex lore, world-building, or non-fiction research without losing their distinct authorial voice.
